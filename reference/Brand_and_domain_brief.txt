Kasparro and its customer problem

Kasparro presents itself as helping sellers measure and improve their presence in AI-assisted commerce. Its website focuses on direct-to-consumer (D2C) brands and product catalogs, and describes four audit areas. [1]

The business question is whether a brand enters the buyer’s consideration set, whether its description is accurate, and what evidence would justify an improvement.

Area | Publicly described focus
Search visibility | Presence in relevant comparative AI answers.
Trust signals | Verifiable claims, citations and supporting proof.
Content quality | Clear catalog information and editorial content.
Transaction readiness | Product information that supports agent-assisted purchases.

The site offers an AI Revenue Diagnostic and an improvement plan. Its public prompt explorer and leaderboard are explicitly illustrative: the brands are fictional and the figures are not measured output. [1]

For these assignments, Kasparro itself is the brand being analysed. Investigate its discovery as a provider of AI visibility services. Commerce examples explain its customers’ use cases; they are not evidence of Kasparro’s own visibility.

Terms used in these assignments

Term | Working meaning
SEO | Search engine optimisation: helping relevant web content be discovered and understood in search.
AEO and GEO | Answer engine optimisation and generative engine optimisation. Here, work on accurate brand representation in AI-generated answers.
Surface | The specific AI product or answer experience being measured. Record its mode and model when available.
Prompt and run | A prompt is the submitted question. A run is a recorded collection of responses and its capture configuration.
Inclusion | A brand appears in an answer. This alone does not establish endorsement, accuracy or business impact.
Citation | A source linked or referenced by an answer. Claim support requires checking the source itself.

How to read AI visibility evidence

An answer can mention a brand, recommend it, criticise it or do several of these at once. Preserve the original text and the exact passage behind each judgment. A score should remain traceable to those observations.

Keep observations separate

Mention: the answer names Kasparro. Recommendation: it presents Kasparro as an option suited to the question. A passing reference is not sufficient evidence of a recommendation.

Position: record an order when comparable options are explicitly ranked or listed. Explain when sequence is ambiguous. A prose mention has no automatic ranking.

Accuracy: compare an answer’s material claims with dated, relevant evidence. A company page establishes what the company says; it may not independently prove a performance claim.

Citation support: open the relevant source when permitted, locate the supporting passage and check whether it supports the specific claim. Record uninspected or inaccessible citations as unverified.

Make counts and denominators visible

Define the set being measured, the handling of failed responses and any weighting. Preserve separate components for inclusion, recommendation, negative characterisation and citation presence. There is no required scoring formula in this brief; justify the formula you choose.

For arithmetic only: suppose 12 requests were attempted, 2 failed and 4 of the 10 valid responses mentioned a brand. Report 4/10 valid responses alongside 10/12 coverage and 2 failures. Treating failed requests as successful observations of absence would conceal a data-quality problem.

Distinguish an observation from an explanation

An observation describes the recorded answers. A hypothesis proposes why that pattern occurred. A remedy proposes an action or investigation and a way to test it. Missing evidence about the cause should remain visible. One captured answer per prompt and engine supports a snapshot; it does not establish a persistent ranking or prove that a proposed fix will work.

Understand discovery without assuming a universal fix

Google says established SEO practices remain relevant to AI Overviews and AI Mode. Useful basics include crawl access, internal links, important content in text and structured data consistent with the page. Google specifies no special AI schema or additional technical requirements for those features, and eligibility does not guarantee inclusion. This guidance concerns Google Search; do not automatically extend it to every engine. [2]

OpenAI distinguishes OAI-SearchBot, used for search discovery, from GPTBot, used for potential model-training content. Their access controls are independent. A search-access check can be relevant to a diagnosis, but it does not establish why a particular answer included or omitted a brand. [3]

Using this context in your assignment

The shared question is: how does Kasparro appear in the supplied AI answers, what evidence supports the findings, and what should be investigated or improved next? Establish the answer from the supplied run data. The website alone cannot establish Kasparro’s current inclusion rate, competitors in answers, or engine-level strengths and weaknesses.

Use a marketing or growth lead as the report reader, supported by an analyst and reviewer. These are assignment personas, rather than a statement about Kasparro’s actual customer organisation.

Before drawing a conclusion

Check the pack’s run IDs, capture dates, exact prompts, engine coverage and response counts. Preserve missing metadata as unknown.

Separate branded prompts from unbranded discovery questions. Being named in a question can change what inclusion means.

Compare the same prompts across engines where possible. Explain incomplete coverage or different conditions before aggregating.

Identify competitors from the actual answers. Connect findings and proposed remedies to response IDs, excerpts and inspected sources.

State what would change your conclusion and how you would test the highest-priority recommendation.

Website exploration and live AI checks

Explore the public website to understand the domain. Your role-specific assignment controls which evidence is permitted for assessed outputs. Where live AI checks are allowed, record the exact question, engine, date and relevant settings, and keep the results separate from the supplied baseline. A new answer is another observation; it does not overwrite a saved response. Paid accounts or fresh engine collection are not prerequisites.

Human review and working boundaries

Recommendations can be useful while remaining uncertain. Preserve the evidence, unresolved questions and exact version presented to a reviewer. Follow your role brief for approval and export controls. Use only permitted data and providers, and keep credentials out of submissions. The assignment authorises analysis and a prototype; it does not authorise website changes, publishing findings, contacting customers or executing remedies.
